# Sources

The seeded Visa historical dataset uses Visa Inc.'s FY2025 Form 10-K for the year ended September 30, 2025.

Primary filing:
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316125000089/v-20250930.htm

Seeded reported figures include:
- FY2023-FY2025 net revenue
- net income
- operating cash flow
- purchases of property, equipment and technology
- FY2025 debt
- FY2025 cash and investment securities

Scenario forecasts, probabilities, WACC, terminal growth, volatility assumptions, correlation assumptions, and the runtime market price are investor inputs rather than reported facts.
